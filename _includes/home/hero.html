I study generative 3D vision: how generative models can recover and represent 3D structure from incomplete visual observations.

I led Bone-to-Shape, a single-image animal reconstruction project combining shape priors, video diffusion and 3D Gaussian Splatting, and I now work on sparse-view scene generation with Xinke Li at City University of Hong Kong. I am especially interested in evidence that generative models learn general visual and 3D representations. Longer term, I aim to build visual world models that predict how scenes change, as a basis for spatial reasoning and embodied agents.

Open to long-term remote research internships. Planning to apply for PhD programs for Fall 2028.
