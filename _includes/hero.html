I study how to recover 3D structure from very little visual evidence, combining generative models with geometric and anatomical priors.

I lead Bone-to-Shape, a provincial innovation project that reconstructs animal geometry from a single image using generated views and body-shape priors. In summer 2026 I was a research intern with Xinke Li at City University of Hong Kong, studying generative priors for 3D reconstruction. In the longer term, I am interested in world models for spatial reasoning and action.

Open to long-term remote research internships. Planning to apply for PhD programs for Fall 2028.
