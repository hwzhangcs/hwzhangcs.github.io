I want multimodal models and agents to reason about the visual world: where things are, how views relate, what lies out of view, and how scenes change over time and under action.

My background is in generative 3D vision. I led Bone-to-Shape, a completed single-image animal reconstruction project using shape priors, video diffusion and 3D Gaussian Splatting. At City University of Hong Kong, I work with Xinke Li on sparse-view 3D scene reconstruction with 3D generative priors. Building on this, I am interested in spatial and temporal reasoning in vision-language models, world models that predict how scenes evolve, and embodied and agentic AI.

Open to long-term remote research internships in multimodal reasoning with vision-language models (VLMs), world models, and embodied AI. Planning to apply for PhD programs for Fall 2028.
