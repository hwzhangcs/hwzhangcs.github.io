I am interested in how multimodal models and agents reason about the physical world: where things are, how views relate, and what lies out of view. I am also interested in generative models that fill in what a model cannot see, and in keeping their predictions consistent with what was observed.

My background is in generative 3D vision, where these questions first came up for me. Bone-to-Shape, the single-image animal reconstruction project I led, treats generated views as model predictions, not new observations. At City University of Hong Kong, I work with Xinke Li on improving the generation process of a generative 3D scene reconstructor for sparse-view reconstruction, and we are preparing a paper on this work. In a team course project on hallucination mitigation, I wrote the preference-data pipeline and the DPO training code for a vision-language model, my first hands-on work with multimodal LLMs.

Open to long-term remote research internships on multimodal reasoning with LLMs and agents, starting December 2026. Planning to apply for PhD programs for Fall 2028.
