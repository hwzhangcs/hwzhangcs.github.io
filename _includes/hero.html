Computer Science undergraduate · Sichuan University

I study how to recover 3D structure from very little visual evidence, combining generative models with geometric and anatomical priors.

My current project, Bone-to-Shape, reconstructs animal geometry from a single image using generated views and body-shape priors. In the longer term, I am interested in world models for spatial reasoning and action.

I am a third-year student in the Top-notch Student Training Program 2.0 (Honors Class), ranked first in my class with a 3.96/4.00 GPA. In summer 2026 I was a research intern at City University of Hong Kong.

Open to long-term remote research internships. Planning to apply for PhD programs for Fall 2028.
