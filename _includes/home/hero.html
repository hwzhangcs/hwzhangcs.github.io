I want multimodal models and embodied agents to understand and predict the 3D world: where things are, how views relate, and how scenes change when an agent acts.

My background is in 3D reconstruction and video generation. I led Bone-to-Shape, a completed single-image animal reconstruction project using shape priors, video diffusion and 3D Gaussian Splatting, and I work on sparse-view scene generation with Xinke Li at City University of Hong Kong. Next, I want to bring this 3D grounding to spatial reasoning in vision-language models and to world models for embodied agents and vision-language-action (VLA) models.

Open to long-term remote research internships. Planning to apply for PhD programs for Fall 2028.
